How to use these pages

  1. Read the lesson part first (the idea, the formula card, the worked examples). Copy the formula card onto your cheat sheet draft in your own handwriting.
  2. Do the problems on paper, with the calculator you'll bring to the exam. Write the formula with numbers plugged in; that line earns marks.
  3. Then open "Answer and solution". If your number differs, compare step by step to find the exact line that went wrong.
  4. Tick "done". Your ticks are saved in this browser, and each page shows your progress at the top.
  5. Exam-level problems carry the 2023 point values. Time yourself on those.

To print a worksheet, print the page: solutions, hints and buttons are hidden automatically.

What the 2023 final looked like

Part 1, theory (30 points, closed book, calculator and cheat sheet): 14 one-point short answers (Gini range, OOB, overfitting, feature importance, gradient boosting learning rate, K-means failure cases, GMM and PCA shapes, nn.Linear, CNN shapes and nn.Conv2d arguments, RNN hidden-state shape); a decision tree Gini question (7); random forest bootstrapping (2); two K-means iterations by hand (3); RNN hidden states h1, h2, h3 by hand (4).

Part 2, coding (50 marks + 5 bonus, open book, no AI tools): clean a messy dataset (missing values, string errors, outliers, units), balance classes, EDA, scaling from scratch, stratified split, two or more classifiers with a metrics table, 5-fold cross-validation, grid search, a confusion matrix from scratch, and feature importance.
